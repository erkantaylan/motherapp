# App icon (2026-10-30, main session)

Made the favicon (`web/public/icon.png`, 512 px) with art-forge: `krea2-turbo-txt2img`, four
seeds, picked the fourth, `cutout --key magenta`, then cropped and resized with PIL. Raw renders are
in `~/Desktop/projects/art-forge/out/motherapp/icon-v1/`. Prompt: flat minimal vector app icon, a
rounded square microchip with circuit traces ending in solder dots, royal blue #3b6fd4 and white,
on a flat magenta background. The concept is a motherboard: a core with tools plugged into it.

## Lessons
- [local] `cutout --key magenta` leaves faint alpha over the whole canvas, so `getbbox()` returns
  the full image. → Threshold the alpha (> 128) before taking the bounding box to crop.
- [both] `web/public/` didn't exist before this. Vite copies it to the root of `web/dist`, and the
  core serves everything in dist, so `/icon.png` works without touching the server.
- [both] Removing the old inline SVG favicon with a plain search for `/>` stopped at the `/>`
  inside the SVG. → Replace the whole line instead.
